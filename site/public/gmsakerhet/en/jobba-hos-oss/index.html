Careers

Become part of GMS.

We are looking for committed guards and safety hosts who want to make a difference in Umeå. With us you get a serious employer and colleagues who have your back.

We are looking for you who have

  • Completed Swedish guard training (VU1 + VU2)
  • Good spoken and written Swedish
  • Orderly finances and a clean criminal record
  • Driving licence B (an advantage)
  • Flexibility on hours: day, evening, night and weekend

We offer

  • A serious employer with clear agreements
  • A safe working environment
  • Long-term cooperation
  • Room to grow in the security industry

How to apply

Send your CV and a few lines about yourself to info@gmsumea.se. Write which role you are applying for in the subject.
