Patents

The right to exclude others from your invention.

A patent gives its owner the right to exclude others from making, using or selling the patented invention.

What can be patented

New. Inventive. Industrially applicable.

A patent shall be granted to any industrially applicable invention which is new and involves an inventive step — whether connected with new industrial products, new industrial processes, or a new application of known industrial processes.

A patent is also granted, independently, for any modification, improvement or addition to a previously patented invention that meets the same criteria — in which case it is granted to the owner of the modification, improvement or addition.

GCC Patent Office

One application. Six Gulf states.

Approved by the GCC Supreme Council in 1992, the GCC Patent Office in Riyadh grants patents that secure protection in Bahrain, Kuwait, Qatar, Oman, Saudi Arabia and the UAE.

Requirements for filing in the GCC
  • POA notarized and legalized up to the Saudi Consulate or any GCC member state consulate.
  • Incorporation certificate, notarized and legalized.
  • Business license, notarized and legalized.
  • Deed of assignment, notarized and legalized.
  • Certified copy of the priority document if the Paris Convention is claimed.
General information
  • Priority may be claimed under the Paris Convention within 12 months from the first filing date.
  • Not a member of the PCT.
  • Period of protection: 20 years, subject to annual annuities payable by 31 March of each year.
  • Arabic translation of the full specification, abstract and claims must be submitted with the application.
  • All required documents may be submitted within 90 days from the filing date.
  • The patentee shall exploit the invention in the GCC member states within 3 years from grant.
