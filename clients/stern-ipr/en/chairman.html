01Biography

More than 12 years advising on global IP portfolios.

Mr. Mahmoud Adel heads STERN IP’s Intellectual Property Practice Group in Egypt — the mother firm of ADEL IP LTD, MA IP LTD, ASHRY & ADEL IP, MAHMOUD ADVOCATES LIMITED and STERN GLOBAL IP LTD. He advises on global trademark and patent portfolio management, global brand protection, intellectual property enforcement, licensing and anti-counterfeiting matters.

He contributed to updating the intellectual property law in 2018, supported by the Trademark Office and local trademark attorneys. He has been registered as a lawyer at the Bar since 2006 and as a trademark and patent attorney at the Ministry of Trade since 2008.

Practice focus

Global trademark and design portfolio management and global brand protection; advice on worldwide corporate branding availability; coordination of global and Egyptian trademark portfolios for various clients.
