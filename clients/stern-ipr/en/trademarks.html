Trademarks in Egypt

Requirements & key facts.

Filing systemSingle & multi-class
ClassificationNice, 11th edition
Filing to certificate12–18 months
Search turnaround30 days
Protection10 years, renewable
Renewal grace6 months · 3 years with super penalty

If there is no Egyptian Consulate in your country, legalization can be made by any Arab consulate.

Trademark application requirements
  • Full name, address and nationality of the applicant.
  • Power of attorney notarized and legalized up to the Egyptian Consulate.
  • Trademark image.
  • List of goods/services to be covered by the application.
  • Certified copy of the priority document if claimed (accepted within six months).
  • The application can be submitted without the POA; the scanned legalized POA must follow within 6 months without additional fees, and the original before receiving the registration certificate.
Trademark renewal requirements
  • Power of attorney notarized and legalized up to the Egyptian Consulate.
  • Scanned copy of the trademark registration certificate.
Trademark assignment requirements
  • Power of attorney signed by the assignee, notarized and legalized up to the Egyptian Consulate.
  • Assignee’s business license / incorporation certificate / commercial registry, notarized and legalized.
  • Assignment contract signed by both parties, or by the assignor at least, notarized and legalized.
  • Copy of the trademark registration certificate.
Change of name / address requirements
  • POA reflecting the new name/address, notarized and legalized up to the Egyptian Consulate.
  • Certificate of change of name/address, notarized and legalized up to the Egyptian Consulate.
