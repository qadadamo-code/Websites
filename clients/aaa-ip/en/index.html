04From filing to protection

The life of an Egyptian trademark.

Six stages, each with its deadline. Our reminder system tracks every one of them for you.

  1. Day 0

    Filing

    Notarized & legalized power of attorney — one POA covers all applications of the same applicant, and may follow within six months at no extra fee.

  2. Examination

    Classification

    International classification of goods & services, with multi-class filing since 2007. Paris Convention priority may be claimed.

  3. Gazette

    Publication

    The accepted mark is published in the Official Trademark Gazette.

  4. 2 months

    Opposition

    A two-month window during which third parties may oppose.

  5. 10 years

    Registration

    Protection for 10 years from the filing date — first to file.

  6. ∞

    Renewal

    Renewed for further 10-year terms; the renewal is filed within 12 months before expiry.
