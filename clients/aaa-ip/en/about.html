IAbout us

One of the most active IP firms in Egypt.

One of the most active IP firms in Egypt & the Middle East — a well-established law firm providing a full spectrum of intellectual property services: trademarks, patents, industrial designs & copyrights. We assist small, medium and large organizations & individuals to establish, protect & enforce their rights in Egypt, the Middle East and all African countries.

Operating from its headquarters in Egypt, AAA IP has offices in many major Arab cities, and liaises with a worldwide network of associates in North America, South America, Africa, Asia and Oceania.
